## Customer vs supplier naming
Customer-facing records use descriptive Print Kings names. Supplier SKU, supplier name and supplier cost are internal fields.

## Current catalogue source
The September 2026 reseller list supplied for Print Kings is authoritative for initial product/cost import.

## Product model
Product → category → variant → supplier mapping → price version → media → specifications → branding options → related products/add-ons.

## Variant principle
Use variants where products share a meaningful product family, e.g. flag shape with size/single/double configurations. Do not create duplicate product records unnecessarily.

## Bundle principle
Packages are first-class bundle records composed of products/variants. They may have their own customer-facing names and merchandising.

## Data quality
Do not infer missing specifications, stock, images, branding limitations or delivery information. Unknown values remain unknown until verified.
