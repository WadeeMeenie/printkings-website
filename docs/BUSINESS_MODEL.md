# Print Kings — Business Model

## Positioning
Print Kings is a customer-facing outdoor branding and event-display solution provider. The business sells complete branded solutions, not merely individual promotional products.

## Core promise
MAKE YOUR BRAND IMPOSSIBLE TO MISS.

## Primary customer segments
- Small businesses
- Market vendors
- Event businesses and organisers
- Sports clubs, schools and tournaments
- Corporate customers
- Roadshow/activation teams
- Retail and hospitality businesses

## Customer buying modes
1. Direct product purchase
2. Build Your Setup
3. Request a quote
4. Assisted sales/contact

## Revenue sources
- Product gross margin
- Bundle/setup margin
- Branding/production margin where applicable
- Delivery/handling where applicable
- Corporate and bulk orders
- Repeat/reorder business

## Commercial rules
- Supplier is Display Mania.
- Supplier SKU and cost data are internal.
- September 2026 reseller pricing is the current commercial source of truth and is valid until 31 October 2026 subject to stock.
- Pricing must be versioned.
- Gross margin, not markup, is the primary commercial metric.
- True cost should eventually include direct inbound, payment, artwork/production and other direct costs.

## Customer promise boundaries
Only make claims that can be supported. Do not claim market leadership, lowest price, guaranteed delivery, stock availability or production capability without evidence.
