# Print Kings — Decisions

## D-001 — Planning before implementation
The project starts with a complete business/product/technical planning gate before application coding.

## D-002 — Build Your Setup is the signature journey
The storefront should solve customer needs through guided configuration rather than relying only on catalogue browsing.

## D-003 — Supplier data is internal
Display Mania is the backend supplier source. Supplier SKUs and costs are not customer-facing by default.

## D-004 — Gross margin is the commercial metric
Pricing decisions use gross margin rather than confusing markup with margin.

## D-005 — Price versioning
Supplier costs and pricing are versioned because supplier lists change over time.

## D-006 — No guessed brand palette
The logo is the visual authority. Exact colours must be verified before being frozen into design tokens.

## D-007 — MVP before advanced visualisation
3D, AR and AI mockups are future capabilities, not launch dependencies.
